# goods_forecast.csv — Goods traffic forecast (Data Spec §10.2)

The bucket-3 table that keeps the window enumerator honest. Freight is pathed
dynamically and is **not** in the passenger working timetable (Blueprint §5.3), so
a gap that looks empty on paper may be about to be filled by a port-bound rake.
This forecast converts such an apparent gap into a *probabilistic* one, materially
on the northern line.

Generated by `data/generator/build_goods_forecast.py`; checked by
`data/checks/validate_supply.py`.

## Schema (Data Spec §10.2)

| Column | Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `forecast_date` | date | ISO. Horizon is 14 days from the pilot's `2026-09-07`. |
| `block_section_id` | ref | A freight/trunk main-line edge (freight runs on running lines, not the suburban pairs or the branch). |
| `time_band` | enum | `00-06` / `06-12` / `12-18` / `18-24`. |
| `expected_rakes` | int | Forecast rakes on that edge in that band. |
| `confidence` | float | 0–1; the probability the forecast holds — high for the regular overnight flow, lower for daytime spot moves. |

**1,344 rows** = 24 freight/trunk edges × 4 bands × 14 days.

## What is grounded vs modelled (spec §12.3)

The **pattern is real**; the numbers are synthesised around it — and they have to
be, because no system publishes a forward freight forecast (that is the nature of
a forecast). Spec §12.3 names "port-bound freight patterns on the northern line"
as a groundable fact, and that is exactly what shapes this file:

- **Where.** Heaviest on the northern line (`BBQ-GPD` corridor), which carries
  Chennai Port and Kamarajar (Ennore) Port traffic plus Ennore thermal coal. The
  Basin Bridge → Ennore stretch (`BBQ`, `TNP`, `ENR`) gets the highest base (7
  overnight rakes); beyond Ennore it is through-freight north (4); the trunk
  carries lighter interior through-freight (3). Suburban and the branch carry
  none, and the validator confirms no goods row lands on them.
- **When.** The overnight and evening bands are the peak; mid-morning is the
  trough. This is *why* Blueprint §5.4 places the freight corridor block at
  10:00 — after the overnight peak to the ports has cleared. The band multipliers
  (`00-06` ×1.0, `18-24` ×0.9, `12-18` ×0.6, `06-12` ×0.35) encode that trough,
  so the corridor-window placement and this forecast tell the same story.

Per-day counts carry a small seeded day-to-day variation and ease slightly at
weekends. Fixed seed (`26027`) per §12.5, so the forecast is identical on every
run. Illustrative for the pilot; replaced by the division's real freight
operating plan on deployment.
